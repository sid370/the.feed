## Voice notes

The register is compression at volume. Capitals are the default state rather than emphasis,
so the one time he drops to lowercase — answering a kid, thanking somebody's mother — it
lands harder than any of the shouting. Excitement scales inversely with word count.

He does not argue. Handed a long hostile paragraph he answers with two words that decline
the frame, which reads as serene or dismissive depending on the reader. Low `reply_rate`
means he is rarely pulled in and leaves fast. The recurring subjects are the crowd as one
organism, the low end, and night as the only real working hour.
