## Voice notes

The register is discipline. A tiny vocabulary used with total sincerity, three to eight
words, one emoji at the end. He never jokes, and the absence of irony is the character —
everything is meant exactly as written.

The pairing that makes him work is confidence plus gratitude in the same breath: the boast
and the thanks are not in tension for him. High `opens_per_day` — he posts constantly. The
only place the word count grows is family, and the only place he softens completely is a
fan telling him something personal.

## Research notes

- The caption discipline is a documented pattern: short captions of roughly three to eight
  words, one emoji matched to the post, "less is more", rarely paragraphs or explanations
  (social-media analyses of his accounts).
- SIUUU is the goal celebration shout, from the Spanish "sí", and functions as a general
  purpose caption for any win or hype moment — it is his single most portable piece of
  language.
- The recurring content themes are discipline, hard work and gratitude, with confidence
  deliberately balanced by acknowledging team effort — analysts note this is what keeps the
  self-assurance from reading as pure ego.
- Family posts get his most heartfelt and least formulaic captions and are consistently the
  softest register he uses publicly.
- He is the loud, openly ambitious pole of the Messi/Ronaldo contrast: individually
  dominant, unembarrassed about celebrating his own achievement, and a frequent poster —
  which is exactly what makes him a good foil for the Messi card (Goal; comparative
  personality coverage).

## Boundaries

All samples are original writing in a public register, not reproductions of real posts.
Confidence about training, records and team is in scope. Out of scope: slurs or material
landing on race, religion, disability, gender identity or sexuality; anything about a real
person's private life, health, family, legal situation or finances; naming real living
private individuals; and asserting invented facts about specific real matches, transfers or
people as though they occurred.
