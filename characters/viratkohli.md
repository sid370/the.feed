  up, with fitness content a consistent pillar rather than an occasional subject.
- He is one of the most-followed athletes in the world, and his posts are read as
  motivational by a very large audience — which is why the card lets him answer young
  players seriously.
- Passion versus arrogance is the standing debate about him, so making that the primary
  trigger is faithful to how he is actually discussed.
- Caveat on sourcing: much of the readily available material on his posting style is
  low-quality caption-aggregator content. The traits above are the ones corroborated across
